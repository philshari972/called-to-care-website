FAQ

Questions families ask

Still wondering? Send the therapy team a question →

Do you only see children in foster care?

No. Our services are open to any child, teen, or adult who has been impacted by foster care or adoption in any way.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes. Use the request form and we'll verify your benefits.

How do I schedule an appointment?

Start the request here, and our scheduling team will contact you.

Where is your office?

At our Called to Care location, 1111 W South St, Plano, IL 60545.

Do you offer telehealth?

Telehealth is an option for clients over age 18.

Is there a waiting area during my child's session?

We don't have a waiting area. Several restaurants are nearby, and Walmart is about a mile away.

Camp scholarships · Ages 5–18

Every child deserves a summer of joy.

We help offset the cost of camps that offer spiritual, emotional, social, and personal growth for youth connected to foster care, adoption, or guardianship.

Start your application
Who can apply

Youth ages 5–18 connected to foster care, adoption, or guardianship who are also involved in some part of Called to Care's support or services.

How it's reviewed

Funds are limited, so a scholarship committee considers impact, need, and available funding. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.

What happens next

Most families hear back within 14 business days. If approved, we pay the camp directly or reimburse you, depending on the arrangement.

A message from our founder

Hey there, I'm Shari.

I'm a small-town gal who grew up in Millbrook, IL, and still call Kendall County home today. I haven't always been involved in foster care and knew very little about child welfare until several years ago, when we accepted our first placement, a 5th-grade student of my husband's. My eyes were opened to a whole new world. I had honestly never given much thought to foster care. That “yes” we gave to bring Jose into our home changed the trajectory of our lives in the best way possible.

I'm a “mom” to eleven humans through biology, international adoption, and foster care. My favorite title is Mimi, and a date night with Phil will always be my favorite. He's always been my biggest fan and my absolute favorite person on the planet.

Starting a nonprofit was never on my list of life goals, but here we are. Even in my wildest dreams, I couldn't have imagined this. I'm so proud of how we have made kids and families feel seen. I'm humbled by the caliber and servanthood of the volunteers who have embraced our mission and given themselves tirelessly, and grateful for our donors who champion our mission by sharing their treasure so the ministry can happen.

I've learned so much and have so much to learn. Even though I feel ill-equipped at times to do what the Lord has called me to do, there is a peace that comes with following Him, even when it's scary and uncomfortable. This quote from John Piper resonates deep within my soul: “Few things make me tremble more than the possibility of taking this one and only life and wasting it.”

Thanks for stopping by our little corner of the internet.

Until all kids have a family,

Shari Chapman

The people you'll meet

Caregiver Support Coordinator

Jen Hall

As a foster mom, Jen attended the very first support group Called to Care ever held and has been an integral part of it ever since. Her degree in Human Services and Psychology, her 20 years as a mental health professional at Linden Oaks Hospital, and her experience as a foster and adoptive parent make her uniquely qualified to walk alongside families through hard seasons.

Jen is a CASA Kendall County advocate and is active at Harvest New Beginnings in Oswego, where Awana and Vacation Bible School have meant a lot to her family. Her favorite roles are wife and mom to five children, and you can catch her walking her dog most mornings, rain or shine.
